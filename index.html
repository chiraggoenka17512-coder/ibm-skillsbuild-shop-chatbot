Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my shipment?
You can use our built-in chatbot! Simply open PulseBot and type or click Track Order. Enter sample order IDs like ORD-101 or ORD-202 to view live simulated delivery tracking!
What is your return & exchange policy?
We offer a 14-day hassle-free return window for unworn items with original tags intact. Exchanges are always 100% free of charge!
Are your clothing items ethically sourced?
Yes, 100% of our collection uses GOTS-certified organic cotton, OEKO-TEX non-toxic dyes, and recycled polyester blends.
